Wishwellfor Steam

The Steam wishlist, a lot more useful.

Wishwell replaces it with a panel for deals away from Steam, how long each game takes, and whether it runs on Steam Deck. And plenty more. It all stays on the page itself.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

It is not published yet. The day it is, this button leads to the listing.

Sort and filter

Filters and sorting across hundreds of games: by discount, by price, by length and by whether it is available.

In other stores

Next to the Steam price, what it costs in other stores and the all-time low. That part needs a free IsThereAnyDeal key, pasted in the options.

How long it takes

HowLongToBeat on every game: the estimated time for the story, the main path and a full completion. If you want it, you can also see what each hour works out to.

Whether it will run

Steam Deck compatibility and the ProtonDB rating, on the card itself.

No accounts, no server

Wishwell has no server of its own and asks you to register for nothing: it reads your public wishlist and queries the data sources straight from your browser.

Deals from other stores and the all-time low show up once you paste a free IsThereAnyDeal key. Filters, length and Steam Deck work without that key.
